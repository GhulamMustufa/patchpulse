# 🎬 120-Second Video Recording Script

Follow this second-by-second guide during your screen recording.

---

### [ 0:00 - 0:15 ] The Hook & The Problem
> **Audio**: "Every single day, [target users] struggle with [painful problem]. Current tools are disjointed and slow. Today, we built **[Project Name]**—an autonomous engine that solves this in under 200 milliseconds."
> **Visual**: Show the home page headline, or a quick visual representation of the problem.

---

### [ 0:15 - 0:45 ] The Magic Moment
> **Audio**: "Let’s see it in action. A judge or user doesn't even need to sign up—they just click 'Explore Live Interactive Demo'. Watch what happens when I trigger the autonomous pipeline..."
> **Visual**: Click the 1-Click Demo button, navigate to Dashboard, click 'Execute Magic Moment Demo'. Show the live terminal log executing in real time.

---

### [ 0:45 - 1:15 ] Deep Workflow & Pre-Seeded Proof
> **Audio**: "Notice how the system handles [key feature 1] and [key feature 2]. Under the hood, we are streaming updates directly to the client with sub-50ms latency, verified against pre-seeded production data."
> **Visual**: Scroll to the verified events table, hover over status badges, show the metric cards.

---

### [ 1:15 - 1:45 ] Tech Stack & Sponsor Callouts
> **Audio**: "To make this possible, we integrated [Sponsor Name] for [feature X], [Sponsor B] for [feature Y], and deployed seamlessly to [Host Platform]. Our repository includes a dedicated SPONSORS.md file with exact line references proving our deep implementation."
> **Visual**: Quick flash of the architecture diagram or the clean code in `src/lib/` showing the sponsor SDK.

---

### [ 1:45 - 2:00 ] Impact & Closing Call-to-Action
> **Audio**: "[Project Name] is live right now at [URL]. You can test the interactive demo yourself with one click. Thank you, and we look forward to your feedback!"
> **Visual**: Final slide with live URL, team names, and GitHub repository QR/link.
